Learning objectives

A student who has met the objectives of the course will be able to:

  • Locate the correct entrance to the building on the first attempt
  • Congratulate the host without asking what the thesis was about
  • Identify at least three people they have never met before
  • Operate a quiz interface under non ideal conditions
  • Document the evening photographically, within the stated upload limit
  • Assess their own capacity accurately, and act on that assessment
