<objective>
Turn the tracer's single path into the transcript a guest can actually read at a party: five
photographs picked at once, one numbered row each, one file moving at a time, a bar that reports
real bytes and stops short of claiming completion before the archive has answered, and a control
whose eight states are all reachable and all named in words.

Purpose: PH-05 forbids a silent failure, and the quiet version of a silent failure is a file the
guest chose that nothing on screen ever mentions again. The queue is what makes every picked file
accounted for, by name and by number, at every moment.

Output: the batch model, the sequential driver, the queue renderer and its seven row states, the
eight control states with their two live regions, the language-switch guard, and the CSS for the
transcript and the 2px bar that doubles as the row's separating rule.
</objective>
