<objective>
Complete the section's answer to every question a guest can arrive with: the site has no credentials
yet, it is three hours before the party, they never registered, or they are ready to upload and want
to know how many they have left. Four of the five bodies, the opening gate that decides between two
of them, and the two things that sit above the button.

in August and collect jokes. The gate also has to be recoverable from a phone: `photos.opensAt: null`
is the owner's one line escape on the night, and an unparseable value must open rather than close.

Output: `photosOpen()` on the countdown's own arithmetic, the closed body with its formatted moment,
the re-evaluation that needs no second clock, the completed ladder including the registration gate,
the remaining-count data field, the permanence line, and the body swap transition with its reduced
motion rule.
</objective>
