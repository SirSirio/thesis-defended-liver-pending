<objective>
Close the second half of the registration act. A guest who has just registered gets one large tap to
the group at the moment they are most willing, a guest who tapped past it finds the link again in a
section that is always there, and everyone sees a head count once it is a number worth showing.

Purpose: the phase's headline sentence ends with "lands in the WhatsApp group with one more tap".
That sentence cannot be demonstrated until the owner supplies a link, and `whatsapp.inviteUrl` is
still null. So this plan builds the linkless state as the real state, not as an edge case, and makes
the configured state one config line away. The social proof half is the persuasion that makes the
form worth filling in, and it is the one thing on the page that reads other guests' data.

Output: a persistent group section that ships hidden, three group CTAs with one writer between them,
and a head count and class list in the fact table's own grammar.
</objective>
