<objective>
Make the nudge bar correct on the devices it is about to appear on. Plan 01 rendered `#enrol-form`,
which switched the bar on for every guest, on every device, for the first time in the life of this
site. Four things that were unobservable until that moment are now live defects: the bottom reserve
is a guess that is short by up to 27px on a notched iPhone, a toast lands on top of the bar, the bar
sits over the soft keyboard while a guest is typing into the form it points at, and dismissing it
jumps the page up under their thumb.

Purpose: NDG-02 says the bar never covers the countdown, the address or the door video. That
sentence has never been checkable, because the bar has never rendered. This plan turns it into a
measured contract and then verifies the rest of the bar rather than rebuilding it, because D-27 is
right that the bar is finished.

Output: a measured `--nudge-h`, a reserve and a toast offset and a scroll padding all composed from
it, a keyboard-aware hide, the two shipped-code corrections the bar's first appearance exposes, and
a recorded audit of NDG-01 through NDG-08 against real behaviour.
</objective>
