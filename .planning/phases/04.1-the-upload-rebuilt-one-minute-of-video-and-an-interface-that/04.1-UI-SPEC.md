# Phase 04.1 UI spec: the upload, rebuilt

**Status:** contract. Anything built in this phase is checked against this file.
**Design skills used as input:** `design-taste`, loaded before this file was written, per the
standing owner instruction in `use-design-skills-phase-5` which says the skills drive the
design rather than audit it afterwards.

---

## Design read

A fake DTU course page for a graduation party. Dark, institutional red, mono inflected. The
register is a bureaucratic form that has had a couple of drinks.

Dials: **DESIGN_VARIANCE 4**, **MOTION_INTENSITY 4**, **VISUAL_DENSITY 5**.

Motion is deliberately low for this one section while the rest of the page misbehaves. The
uploader is the only place on the site where the guest has a **task** with a failure mode and
a byte cost. A control that jokes while it is losing your video is not funny.

---

## The governing idea

**The uploader joins the awakening it was never part of.**

`:root[data-awake="1"]` already takes `--r-sm` 2 to 10px, `--r-md` 8 to 18px, `--r-lg` 10 to
28px, and `--glass` and `--lift` 0 to 1, over 1.0 to 1.4 seconds. That transition is the
page's central visual joke: the institutional mask is square, and it softens.

The uploader today declares no radius on its container and reaches for `--r-sm` on its primary
button, so it is the one block that stays hard while the page around it softens. Every rule
below is a consequence of fixing that, and **no rule below introduces a new custom property or
a hardcoded radius.** If a value is not a token, it is wrong.

This is also what protects the joke. The owner has repeatedly protected the DTU parody, and
the answer to "more modern, more rounded" is not a second rounded style bolted next to the
first. It is the block finally speaking the language the page already speaks.

---

## 1. The card

`.uploader` stops being a naked stack and becomes a surface.

| Property | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `background` | `var(--surface)` | It is a container now, so it needs to sit on something. |
| `border` | `1px solid var(--rule)` | Same edge every other surface on the page carries. |
| `border-radius` | `var(--r-md)` | 8px institutional, 18px awake. **Matches `.mine__tile` exactly**, so the card and the thumbnails under it are one family. |
| `padding` | `var(--s-5)` | 24px, dropping to `var(--s-4)` below 400px. |
| `box-shadow` | `0 10px 26px rgba(0, 0, 0, calc(0.34 * var(--lift)))` | The exact expression already used at `styles.css:3421`. Copied rather than invented so there is one lift value on the page. |

**`--r-lg`, 28px awake, is deliberately not used.** A 28px radius on a full width card at
390px reads as a consumer app and loses the syllabus. 18px is soft and still institutional.

**No nested cards.** The queue rows, the chips and the allowance meter sit *on* this card,
never in cards of their own. Nested cards are always wrong, and this section is where the
temptation is highest because there are already five sub blocks in it.

---

## 2. The allowance, as a meter rather than a definition list

Today the remaining count is a `dl.facts.facts--record` reading "Submissions remaining / 3".
It is accurate, and it is a spreadsheet.

Replace with **five pips in a row**, one per slot, keeping the number for screen readers and
for anyone who wants the digit.

```
YOUR ALLOWANCE
[####] [####] [####] [    ] [    ]        3 of 5 used
 photo  photo  video   free   free
```

- Each pip is a `--r-sm` rounded block, `--s-2` gap, filled `--ink-dim` when spent and
  `--rule` outlined when free.
- The pip holding a video is filled `--accent-lit` and carries a play glyph, so a guest can
  see at a glance that their one video is gone.
- The digit line stays, in mono with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.
- The pips are `aria-hidden` with a single correct sentence beside them. Five divs announced
  one at a time is worse than the sentence.

**Motion:** a pip fills with `transform: scale(0.95)` to `1` plus opacity over `--t-fast`.
Never from `scale(0)`. Under `prefers-reduced-motion` it changes colour with no transform.

## 3. The rules, stated explicitly and big

The owner asked for the video limit to be "written explicitely and big". This is that, and it
sits above the picker rather than only inside a refusal, because a rule a guest reads after
being refused is a rule that arrived too late.

A single strip inside the card, directly under the allowance meter:

```
5 FILES  .  ONE MAY BE A VIDEO  .  60 SECONDS  .  50 MB
```

- Mono, `--font-mono`, letter spacing `0.04em`, uppercase.
- **`60 SECONDS` is the emphasised term**: `--ink` at 600 weight, where the rest of the strip
  is `--ink-dim` at 400. That is the explicit and big the owner asked for, achieved with
  weight and colour contrast rather than by making one line of a four line block physically
  large, which would unbalance the card.
- Wraps to two lines below 420px. The separators are middots in `--ink-faint`, so the strip
  reads as one sentence rather than four fields.
- Every number in it comes from `config.js`. **No number is written into `copy.js`.** The note
  at `config.js:216` warns that spelling numbers into copy is a three file trap, and this
  strip must not add a fourth instance of it.

## 4. The picker becomes a drop zone

Today it is a full width red button and nothing else. It becomes a **target with a button
inside it**, which is both more modern and strictly more capable, because a desktop guest can
drag files onto it.

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| `border` | `2px dashed var(--rule-lit)` |
| `border-radius` | `var(--r-md)` |
| `background` | transparent, going to `rgba(255,255,255,0.03)` on drag over |
| `padding` | `var(--s-6)` block, `var(--s-4)` inline |
| min height | 132px, so it is unmistakably a target and not a divider |

- The whole zone is clickable and opens the same picker the button does.
- The primary button **moves from `--r-sm` to `--r-md`**. This is the single most visible fix
  in the phase: the most important control on the page currently carries the smallest radius
  in the system.
- On drag over the dash goes `--accent-lit` and the background lifts. On drop it returns.
  Transform and opacity only, `--t-fast`, `--ease-out`.
- Touch targets: the button keeps its 52px floor and 56px on a coarse pointer. The zone being
  tappable is an addition, never a replacement, because a dashed rectangle is not an obvious
  button to everyone and the button has to survive on its own.
- **Drag and drop is desktop sugar and is never the only route.** The picker input stays
  exactly as it is, including the `accept` rules in section 6.

## 5. The feedback text becomes chips

This is the block the owner called "the comments", and it is the least designed thing in the
section: bare 13px mono log lines separated by 2px hairlines.

### The queue row

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| `background` | `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` |
| `border-radius` | `var(--r-sm)`, 2px institutional and 10px awake |
| `padding` | `var(--s-3)` |
| gap between rows | `var(--s-2)`, replacing the hairline rules |

The state word stops being plain text and becomes a **pill chip**:

| State | Chip background | Chip text | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| `waiting` | `rgba(255,255,255,0.06)` | `--ink-dim` | |
| `preparing`, `uploading`, `recording` | `rgba(232,63,72,0.14)` | `--accent-lit` | in flight |
| `done` | `rgba(255,255,255,0.06)` | `--ink-dim` | not green, there is no green on this site |
| `refused`, `failed` | `rgba(232,63,72,0.14)` | `--accent-lit` | |

Chips are `border-radius: 999px`, `--font-mono`, 12px, `padding: 3px 10px`, uppercase,
`letter-spacing: 0.04em`.

**The refusal reason stays a full sentence in `--accent-lit` under the name.** It is not
chipped and it is not truncated. It is the one piece of text in this block that has to be read
and acted on, and `photo-rejections-unexplained` is an open bug precisely because a guest
could not tell why a file was refused.

### The progress bar

The existing 2px hairline is documented as doing double duty: it is the row's progress track
**and** its separating rule, and the comment argues it must never change height because every
row below would step sideways mid batch.

That argument dies the moment rows are separated by a gap instead of a rule, so the change is
deliberate and this is the record of it. The bar becomes a **4px inset rounded track**,
`border-radius: 999px`, `background: var(--rule)`, sitting inside the row's padding, with the
fill on the same 999px.

The fill keeps every property that made it correct: `transform: scaleX()` from a left origin
and never `width`; `linear` easing, because the quantity is linear and easing a real
measurement makes it lie; `--accent-lit` in flight and `--ink-dim` when done.

### The status and alert lines

`.uploader__status` becomes a chip on the same 999px pill, `--ink-dim` on
`rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`, so the polite line reads as a badge rather than as stray text.

**Its reserved box survives.** The current min height exists so the button and the album below
do not step 24px the moment a batch starts, under the thumb that started it. The chip must
reserve the same space in every state or that no reflow guarantee is lost.

`.uploader__alert` stays borderless `--accent-lit` text. It is deliberately **not** a chip and
deliberately not a bordered box: a bordered red box would be the only one on the page and
would read as a browser error rather than as this site. That reasoning is already in the
stylesheet and it still holds.

## 6. Video

### In the picker

- `accept` becomes `image/*,video/*`. **The image half is not touched.** Adding `image/heic`
  makes Safari hand back a real HEIC that Android Chrome cannot decode, and that trap is
  documented at `app.js` around line 4925. Widening for video must not widen for HEIC.
- A video is validated on **size first, then duration**, never the other way round. Size is
  cheap; duration requires handing the file to a `video` element and waiting for
  `loadedmetadata`, which is the video equivalent of the validate before you decode rule the
  image path already follows.
- Refusals must name the real reason and be actionable:
  - over 60s: say the length picked and the length allowed.
  - over 50 MB: say to film at a lower quality. "Too large" alone is not something a guest can
    act on at a party.
  - a second video: say the allowance is one, not that the file is wrong.

### In the strip and the album

- The thumbnail is the video's own first frame, obtained with a `#t=0.1` media fragment on a
  `video` element with `preload="metadata" playsinline muted`. **No separate poster object is
  uploaded.** There is no build step and Storage does not thumbnail on the free tier, so a
  second uploaded object is the only alternative and it doubles the write path for one frame.
- **The fallback is mandatory and must be built, not assumed.** iOS Safari does not reliably
  paint the fragment frame before interaction. When no frame paints, the tile shows a
  `--surface` panel with the play glyph and the duration, which is a designed state rather
  than a black rectangle.
- A video tile carries a **duration badge**, bottom right, 999px pill, mono, tabular figures,
  on `rgba(11,11,12,0.72)` so it survives any frame.
- A video tile carries a **play glyph**, centred, in a 999px disc.
- **Nothing autoplays in the mosaic.** The album is a grid on the phone that page exists for,
  and six autoplaying videos is a frame rate problem and a data bill. Playback happens in the
  lightbox, on a tap, with controls.

## 7. States

All eight, for the drop zone and for every chip.

| State | Requirement |
|---|---|
| default | as specified above |
| hover | `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)` only. Zone border goes `--rule-lit` to `--accent-lit`. |
| focus | `:focus-visible` ring, never `outline: none` without a replacement. The zone is focusable because it is clickable. |
| active | `transform: scale(0.97)` on the button. The zone itself does not scale, because it contains the button and nesting two scales looks broken. |
| disabled | in flight the zone loses its dash, the button dims, and the zone stops being a click target. It must be visibly not a target, not merely inert. |
| loading | the queue is the loading state. The zone shows the count in flight. |
| error | the alert line plus per row chips. |
| success | chips go `done`, the status chip carries the count, pips fill. |

## 8. Non negotiables carried in

- **Zero em dashes** in anything a guest sees, in all three languages.
- **Copy parity**: every new key exists in en, it and da. Currently 246 each.
- **No new custom property** is declared by this work, and **no hardcoded radius**. Every
  radius is `--r-sm`, `--r-md`, or `999px` for pills.
- **`prefers-reduced-motion`** removes every transform in this block and keeps colour and
  opacity changes, which carry meaning.
- **Contrast**: `--ink-dim` is 7.65:1 on `--bg` and stays legible on `--surface`. Chip text on
  chip background must be re verified after build, not assumed, because the chip backgrounds
  are new surfaces no contrast check has been run against.
- **`storagePath()`, `STORAGE_PATH_RE` in `app.js`, and the second copy of that regex at
  `album.js:139`** change together in one commit, or every new upload becomes invisible.

---

## 9. What this spec deliberately refuses

- **A second rounded language.** Everything rides the existing awakening.
- **Green for success.** There is no green on this site, and adding one for a checkmark would
  be the only green pixel on the page.
- **A bordered red error box.** The reasoning is already in the stylesheet and still correct.
- **Client side video re encoding.** No build step, no packages. Costed and refused in
  `04.1-CONTEXT.md` D-5.
- **Autoplaying video in the mosaic.** Frame rate and data.
- **Icons for their own sake.** The only glyphs are play and the duration badge, both of which
  carry information a word would carry worse.
