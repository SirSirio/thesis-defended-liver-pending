# Plan 04.1-01: the upload control stops being a form

**Goal:** the upload block joins the page's radius awakening and its feedback text stops being
log lines. No Supabase change, no video, no schema. This plan is shippable on its own and is
deliberately first, so the owner sees the direction before the video work is built on top of
it. The Iron Law: this is a draft to be critiqued.

**Contract:** `04.1-UI-SPEC.md` sections 1 to 5 and 7 to 9.

---

## Why this order

The video work in plan 2 changes the same five functions this plan restyles. Doing video
first means restyling code that is about to be rewritten, and doing the visual pass first
means the owner can say "no, not like that" before 300 lines of pipeline work is committed
against the wrong shape.

---

## Tasks

### T1. The card, and the button that carries the wrong radius

`styles.css`, the `PHOTOS: THE UPLOAD CONTROL` block.

- `.uploader` gains `background: var(--surface)`, `border: 1px solid var(--rule)`,
  `border-radius: var(--r-md)`, `padding: var(--s-5)`, and the `--lift` shadow expression
  copied verbatim from `styles.css:3421`.
- Below 400px the padding steps to `var(--s-4)`.
- `.uploader__acts .btn` moves from the inherited `--r-sm` to `--r-md`.
- Write the comment explaining that this block previously declared no radius at all and was
  therefore the one block that did not awaken. The next person to read this file needs the
  reason, not the value.

**Verify:** computed `border-radius` on `.uploader` reads `8px` with `data-awake` absent and
`18px` with it set. The button reads `18px` awake, not `10px`.

**Commit:** `feat(photos) the uploader becomes a card and joins the awakening`

### T2. The allowance meter

`app.js` `buildUploader()` and `syncUploaderLanguage()`, plus `styles.css`.

- Replace the `dl.facts.facts--record` with a `.uploader__allow` block: a pip row plus the
  digit line.
- Pips are built from `maxPerGuest`, read from config, never a literal 5.
- Pips are `aria-hidden="true"`. The sentence beside them is the accessible name.
- The pip fill transition is `transform` and `opacity` only, `--t-fast`, and is removed under
  `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- The video pip variant is built now but nothing sets it until plan 2. It must render
  correctly when asked, and it must not appear on its own.

**Verify:** at 0, 3 and 5 used, the pip count and the digit agree, and the digit is the same
number the old `dl` showed. Screen reader announces one sentence, not five items.

**Commit:** `feat(photos) the allowance is five pips rather than a spreadsheet row`

### T3. The rules strip

`app.js`, `copy.js`, `styles.css`.

- New `.uploader__rules` strip under the meter, built from config values.
- Copy keys carry the sentence structure with `{n}` placeholders; **the numbers are
  substituted from config at render time and never written into `copy.js`.** See
  `config.js:216` for why that trap is worth avoiding a fourth time.
- The video and duration terms render now with the values plan 2 will enforce, so the strip is
  not rewritten twice. **This means the strip states a video rule the code does not yet
  enforce, which is a lie for exactly as long as plan 2 is unmerged.** Therefore: the video
  and duration terms are gated behind `config.photos.video.enabled`, which is `false` in this
  plan and flipped to `true` in plan 2. With it false the strip reads "5 FILES . 12 MB" and
  is true.
- `60 SECONDS` gets `--ink` and 600 weight against the strip's `--ink-dim` 400.
- Three languages, at parity.

**Verify:** with `video.enabled: false` the strip names no video. Flipping it to true in the
console adds the two terms and nothing else moves. No em dashes in any of the three.

**Commit:** `feat(photos) the limits are stated above the picker rather than inside a refusal`

### T4. The drop zone

`app.js` `buildUploader()`, `styles.css`.

- Wrap the button in `.uploader__zone`, 2px dashed `--rule-lit`, `--r-md`, min height 132px.
- Zone click opens the same input. Zone is focusable and has a `:focus-visible` ring.
- `dragover`, `dragleave`, `drop` handlers on the zone; `drop` routes into the same
  `runBatch(files)` the input change handler uses, so there is one entry point and not two.
- **`dragover` must call `preventDefault()` or the browser navigates away to the dropped
  file.** This is the single most common way this feature ships broken.
- Drag and drop is added only where a fine pointer exists. On a phone the zone is a tap
  target and nothing else.
- In flight, the zone loses its dash and stops being a click target.

**Verify:** drop a file on desktop and it uploads through the same path as the picker.
Dropping a file outside the zone does not navigate the page away. On a phone, tapping
anywhere in the zone opens the picker.

**Commit:** `feat(photos) the picker becomes a target you can drop onto`

### T5. The chips

`styles.css`, `app.js` `queueRow()` and the status writer.

- `.queue__row` gains the soft background, `--r-sm`, `--s-3` padding, `--s-2` gap, and loses
  the hairline separation.
- `.queue__state` becomes `.queue__chip`, 999px, mono 12px, uppercase, per the spec's state
  table.
- `.queue__bar` becomes a 4px inset 999px track inside the padding. The fill keeps `scaleX`
  from a left origin, `linear`, and its two colours.
- `.uploader__status` becomes a 999px chip and **keeps its reserved min height in every
  state**, including empty, or the no reflow guarantee is lost.
- `.uploader__alert` is not touched. It stays borderless red text for the reason already
  written in the stylesheet.

**Verify:** run a real batch and watch that no element below the queue moves vertically when
the first row appears or when the status line fills. Contrast check every chip's text against
its own background and record the numbers.

**Commit:** `feat(photos) the transcript reads as chips rather than as log lines`
